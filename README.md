## How to use these references

1. Complete and submit your own homework in Discord's respective homework channels.
2. Re-read the assignment requirements before looking at a reference.
3. Pick a submission based on the specific technique you want to study.
4. Compare the author’s decisions, evidence, and tradeoffs with your own work.
5. Carry the lesson into a new problem instead of copying an artifact.

Good questions to ask while reviewing a submission:

- What evidence led the author to this conclusion?
- Which decisions were automated, and which required human judgment?
- How did the author test the boundary of a failure mode or evaluator?
- What would change if the dataset, policy, or model changed?

## What 'selected' means

The collection is curated by the TA, Antaripa Saha after going through all the homeworks. A submission may be included because it offers strong evidence, a particularly clear explanation, a useful tool, an unusual method, or an honest negative result.

These are references and examples rather than answer keys, and several good submissions may reach different conclusions.

## Links and preservation

Student code stays in the student’s repository and is linked at a specific pull request or commit when available. Video links currently point to the original submission.
